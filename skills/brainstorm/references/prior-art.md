# Prior art: skill `brainstorm`

Date: 2026-11-06. One line per source: what it does, the idea taken, the link.

## Skills and agents

- **obra/superpowers `brainstorming`** (Jesse Vincent, MIT): the agent finds out what the human wants and why before
  building. Taken: one question at a time, 2-3 approaches with trade-offs and a recommendation in a message of their
  own, approve the design in short sections, do not brainstorm a request that is already clear.
  https://github.com/obra/superpowers/blob/main/skills/brainstorming/SKILL.md
- **Hermes Agent** skills hub (`hermes skills search brainstorm`): `official/creative/creative-ideation` generates ideas
  with named methods; `skills-sh/neolabhq/context-engineering-kit/brainstorm` is a community one. Taken: name the
  method used instead of free-form ideation. Nothing copied.
- **clawplays/ospec** (`Brainstorm-First`): surface open decisions one at a time before locking a design; prefer a
  durable required decision over a silent assumption. Taken: the journal records each question and the choice.
  https://github.com/clawplays/ospec
- **joyehuang/skills `brainstorm`** (a "secondary development direction discovery"): narrower (what to build next
  on a project). Not taken; checked for name clashes.
  https://github.com/joyehuang/skills
- **Anthropic skills** and the **Agent Skills spec**: frontmatter rules (description under 1024 chars, triggers).
  https://github.com/anthropics/skills, https://agentskills.io/specification

## The literature (names used as they are)

- **Brainstorming**: Alex F. Osborn, *Applied Imagination* (1953).
- **Double Diamond**: UK Design Council (2005); discover, define, develop, deliver (diverge, converge, twice).
- **Cynefin**: Snowden and Boone, "A Leader's Framework for Decision Making", HBR (2007).
- **Causal loop diagrams**: Forrester, *Industrial Dynamics* (1961); Senge, *The Fifth Discipline* (1990).
- **Leverage points**: Meadows, "Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System" (1999); *Thinking in Systems* (2008).
- **Iceberg model**: attribution uncertain, see the README.

## What is ours

The journal with causal-loop events and the Brainstorm domain in the dev-console, the hand-off to backbrief, and the
rule to use each tool where it fits instead of all of them every time.
